Charitable Objectives & Field Programs

Targeted humanitarian interventions focused on senior citizen welfare, preventive healthcare, and poverty relief.

Nutrition Program

Food & Nutrition for Destitute Elders

Malnutrition is an acute threat for lonely, impoverished elders who lack the stamina to fetch supplies or cook meals. Messiyah Mission Trust conducts direct cooked meal distributions, dry grocery kits, and monthly essential grain packets.

  • Freshly prepared hot meals served directly to elderly people in their homes.
  • Essential provisions: rice, lentils, healthy cooking oils, and spices.
  • Special feast gatherings and festive meals organized to celebrate World Elders Day and community milestones.

Doorstep nutritional food support given directly to an elderly beneficiary.

Preventive Healthcare

Free Eye Clinics & Health Screenings

Vision impairment and unmonitored chronic diseases frequently leave senior citizens incapacitated. We organize regular free ophthalmic and general medical camps in rural hamlets.

  • Comprehensive eye examinations conducted by trained optometrists and doctors.
  • Identification of cataract cases with guidance and facilitation for surgical restoration.
  • Vital screening: Blood pressure, sugar, joint ailments, and general geriatric consultation.

Free eye examination camp conducted for rural senior citizens.

Poverty Relief

Livelihood Assistance & Hardship Relief

Beyond residential care, Messiyah Mission Trust actively empowers vulnerable rural households that care for elderly family members, giving them sustainable means to generate income.

  • Livestock Distribution: Providing dairy/milch animals and goats to underprivileged households to foster self-sufficiency.
  • Relief Distribution: Bedding, blankets, sarees, dhotis, and emergency supplies during harsh monsoon or cold seasons.
  • Direct Compassion Aid: Financial assistance for critical medications and urgent necessities.

Livestock distribution program to empower economically disadvantaged families.
