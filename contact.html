Elders Home Admission Process

Admission to Messiyah Mission Elders Home is open to destitute, abandoned, or isolated senior citizens who lack family guardians.

Please contact our Managing Trustee by telephone at +91 9488912650 or email mairarjcmm@gmail.com with background details of the elderly applicant.

Visiting & Consultation Protocol

To ensure serenity, routine medical rest, and nutritional hygiene for resident elders, all personal visits and donor consultations are conducted by prior appointment.

Please call in advance to coordinate your visit date and time.
