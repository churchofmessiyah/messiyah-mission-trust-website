Frequently Asked Questions

Donation & Legal Clarity

Are donations to Messiyah Mission Trust tax-exempt?

Yes. Messiyah Mission Trust is certified under Section 80G of the Indian Income Tax Act (Certificate No: ITBA/EXM/S/80G/2019-20/1025034868 (1)). Donors in India can claim a 50% tax deduction on their taxable income for eligible contributions.

Can companies partner with the trust for CSR activities?

Yes. The trust is registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs for Corporate Social Responsibility under Registration No. CSR00059066. Corporate entities can direct their CSR eldercare and public health allocations to our programs.

Why are there two bank account entries?

MESSIYAH MISSION TRUST (A/C: 6852039262) is the primary account for general trust operations, while MESSIYAH MISSION OLD AGE HOME (A/C: 6850725899 at Indian Bank, Kandanvilai) is the dedicated branch account specifically utilized for running the elders home, daily meals, and resident medical care.
